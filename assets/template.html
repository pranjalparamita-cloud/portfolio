📌 Myth vs. reality

"You're a student, so mostly theory, right?"

I've worked with real datasets across three internships and shipped interactive Streamlit apps. Theory is great; I prefer it with a pulse.

"Dashboards are just pretty charts."

Pretty is easy. Useful is hard. I start with the decision, then work backwards to the visual.

"RAG is just a fancy search bar."

Not quite — retrieval quality, chunking, similarity routing and evaluation decide whether it's helpful or confidently wrong. I built one that hits 98% syllabus alignment.

"You must love messy data."

I respect it deeply. Like a toddler's drawing: meaningful, chaotic, and definitely needs interpretation.
